Privacy Policy

GRE Vocabulary (Android app com.gauroinc.grevocabulary)
Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information the GRE Vocabulary app ("the app", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your learning progress, favorites, quiz results and settings (such as language and voice speed) are saved in a database on your device only. We do not upload this data to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only for pronunciation features (for example, "say the word" on flashcards). When you tap the microphone, your speech is passed to your device's speech recognition service, which turns it into text so the app can check your pronunciation. Depending on your device, that service (for example, Google's) may process the audio online under its own privacy policy. The app does not record, store or upload your audio itself. You can deny or revoke microphone permission at any time in Android settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and how you interact with ads, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is intended for students and adults preparing for exams and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Because your progress stays on your device, we do not keep copies of it. Information collected by the third-party services above is kept according to their own policies.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The "Last updated" date above will show when it was last revised.
